Protecting your personal information is a core principle at Dungreencrue. We are dedicated to the secure and ethical management of the data entrusted to us by our users. This Privacy Policy outlines the categories of data we gather, how we utilize this information, and the protective measures we have established to safeguard your details. Our practices are fully compliant with relevant U.S. privacy legislation, ensuring complete transparency about our data handling.

Data We Gather

When you engage with our offerings, we may acquire personal identifying details, including but not limited to your full name, postal address, email address, and telephone number. Furthermore, we compile information concerning your website activity, such as device identifiers and your IP address.

Utilization of Your Data

The personal data we gather serves multiple purposes: enabling the provision and enhancement of our services, facilitating transaction processing, establishing communication with you, and generally improving your overall experience. With your explicit permission, we might occasionally employ your information for promotional outreach.

Disclosure of Your Data

Under no circumstances do we engage in the sale or rental of your personal details to external entities. We may, however, share your data with carefully selected third-party service providers who assist us in operating our business, under the strict condition that they commit to safeguarding your information consistent with this policy. Disclosure may also occur if mandated by law or deemed necessary to uphold our legal rights and ensure safety.

Your Rights: Accessing, Amending, or Removing Your Information

You possess the prerogative to review, correct, or request the erasure of any personal data Dungreencrue maintains about you. To activate these entitlements, kindly reach out to our dedicated support team. We pledge to address such requests promptly and in full compliance with all prevailing legal mandates, thereby upholding your privacy rights.

Information Security Measures

We employ stringent security protocols designed to shield your personal details from unauthorized interception or improper use. Our protective framework incorporates advanced encryption techniques, network firewalls, and strict access management, all dedicated to preserving the confidentiality and integrity of our users' information.

Data Retention Period

We will keep your personal information for the duration required to accomplish the objectives stated in this policy, to adhere to our legal responsibilities, or to settle any disagreements. Once personal data is no longer necessary, we guarantee its secure removal or anonymization.

Revisions to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may undergo revisions periodically to incorporate alterations in our operational methods or to meet new legal mandates. We advise you to review this document routinely.
