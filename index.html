Professional garden care all year round

A beautiful and well-maintained garden is a pleasant space to spend time in, relax, welcome guests, and enjoy nature. However, plants, lawns, shrubs, and trees require regular attention. Without timely pruning, soil care, and seasonal maintenance, even a well-designed area will gradually lose its neat appearance.

Dungreencrue offers professional garden and property maintenance services, helping to keep plants healthy, lawns tidy, and the entire property harmonious and well-maintained.

We undertake both regular gardening tasks and individual projects. Our service format is tailored to the size of the property, plant characteristics, season, and current condition of the garden.

A comprehensive approach to your garden

Every property is unique. Some areas require the primary focus on lawns and hedges, while others favor ornamental plants, flower beds, or mature trees. That's why work begins with an assessment of the area and determining the necessary measures.

Dungreencrue specialists pay close attention to detail and strive to preserve the natural beauty of the garden, while making it more tidy and convenient for everyday use.

Services may include:

  • Regular lawn care includes mowing, edging and maintaining a neat shape.
  • Pruning shrubs is the removal of excess branches and the formation of a beautiful appearance of plants.
  • Hedge care – trimming and maintaining the desired height and shape.
  • Seasonal cleaning of the site - removal of leaves, branches and other plant debris.
  • Working with flower beds and flower gardens - maintaining order around ornamental plants.
  • Soil care – loosening and preparing individual areas for planting or seasonal work.
  • Weed removal is the careful clearing of necessary areas of the garden.
  • Preparing the territory for the new season is a complex of works taking into account the time of year.

If necessary, the list of works can be adapted to a specific site.

A well-groomed lawn without unnecessary worries

Lawns occupy a significant portion of many gardens and largely determine the overall appearance of the area. Even small patches of grass require regular trimming and attention.

Professional maintenance helps maintain a uniform grass height and clear lawn edges, preventing untidy areas. Particular attention is paid to areas near paths, fences, trees, and decorative elements.

Dungreencrue can carry out lawn maintenance on a regular basis or as part of a comprehensive full-site care service.

Trimming shrubs and hedges

Overgrown shrubs can quickly change the appearance of a garden. They can block paths, interfere with other plants, and create a feeling of neglect.

Timely pruning helps:

  • maintain the neat shape of the bushes;
  • control the size of plants;
  • remove dry and unnecessary branches;
  • keep free space near paths;
  • maintain general order on the site.

Particular attention is paid to hedges, as they require regular maintenance to maintain an even geometry.

Seasonal maintenance of the territory

The garden changes with the seasons, so the list of necessary works also differs throughout the year.

In spring, the area needs cleaning after the cold period, preparing the lawn and tidying up the plants.

In summer, the focus is on regularly mowing the lawn, controlling the growth of shrubs, caring for flower beds, and maintaining the tidiness of the area.

In the fall, an important part of maintenance is clearing fallen leaves, removing plant debris, and preparing the area for the cold season.

This approach allows you to avoid accumulating a large amount of work and keep the garden well-groomed throughout the year.

Regular maintenance or one-time visit

Not every garden requires the same frequency of maintenance. That's why Dungreencrue offers a flexible approach to managing the work .

You can book a one-time visit when you need to tidy up your property after a long break, perform seasonal cleaning, trim a hedge, or solve another specific problem.

For areas requiring constant upkeep, regular maintenance can be arranged. This ensures that key tasks are performed at appropriate intervals, ensuring the area maintains a neat appearance.

The regular format is especially convenient for owners who want to enjoy their garden without wasting free time on constant trimming, cleaning, and other labor-intensive tasks.

Why Professional Care Matters

A garden is a living system that constantly evolves. Grass grows, shrubs expand, weeds appear, and the changing seasons bring new challenges.

If you put off maintenance, small jobs can gradually turn into major cleaning jobs. Regular maintenance helps prevent this and keep the area in good condition without the need for constant major restoration.

Professional service offers several important benefits:

  • Save time – the main gardening work is done by specialists.
  • Stable result – the area is regularly tidied up.
  • Neat appearance - lawns, shrubs and garden areas look well-groomed.
  • Seasonal approach - work is carried out taking into account the current time of year.
  • Individual range of services – services are adapted to the specific needs of a particular site.
  • Comprehensiveness - instead of searching for different contractors, the main gardening tasks can be combined into one service.

Attention to every part of the site

A well-maintained garden is made up of many details. It's not enough to just mow the lawn if the hedges are out of shape, the paths are lined with weeds, and leaves have accumulated under the bushes.

That's why the Dungreencrue team approaches the area holistically . They pay attention not only to individual plants but also to how harmonious the entire site will look once the work is completed.

We strive to ensure that the garden remains natural, neat and comfortable, without the feeling of being overly artificial.

Maintenance of private and other areas

Professional garden maintenance is suitable for a variety of properties. These include small private gardens, spacious areas adjacent to homes, courtyards, green areas adjacent to properties, and other areas requiring regular upkeep.

The scope of work is determined individually. For a small garden, occasional trimming of the lawn and shrubs may be sufficient, while a larger area may require comprehensive maintenance of several areas.

Dungreencrue selects the optimal work format based on the actual needs of the site, without overloading the service with unnecessary procedures.
